FAQs

Common questions about the X Factor career guidance test.

How can this test help me choose the right career path?

It highlights career themes that match your interests and preferred activities. A counsellor can then connect those themes with subjects, courses, skills and realistic career options.

Is there any negative marking?

No. This is a preference-based career assessment. There are no right or wrong answers, so answer honestly.

Do I get a report?

The free test gives an instant insights card on this page. You can contact X Factor for a detailed career fitment blueprint and counselling session.

Can I retake the test later?

Yes. Your interests and confidence can change over time, especially after new subjects, projects or internships. Retaking the test can help you compare your direction.
